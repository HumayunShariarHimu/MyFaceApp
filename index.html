ABOUT THE STUDIO

A private portrait and comparison workspace.

The app estimates a textured 3D face surface from your photo, animates subtle eye movement, and creates a face descriptor locally for one-to-one comparison. Your image and live camera frames are not uploaded by this app.

Open this device’s camera to compare one live frame with your uploaded portrait. The score is for demonstration only and is not certified identity verification or liveness detection.
